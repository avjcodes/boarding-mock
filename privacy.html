What is stored.

  1. No cookies

    This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from advertising or analytics companies. Its fonts, pictures and footage are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

  2. In your browser

    Up to four small notes sit in your browser, and none of them is sent anywhere. Your answer to the age question is kept for 30 days, so you are not asked on every page. If you use the Motion switch, your choice is kept as well. A note of which door you used between pages lasts only until the next page opens. If the sign-up window, or the small bar that stands in for it on a phone, has shown itself, or you have closed it or used it, a note of that is kept so it does not open by itself again for 30 days (a year, once you have used it). Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

  3. Your email

    If you email info@spaceweedusa.com, your message is handled by the Space Weed team. The contact form (on the Contact page, at the foot of the Partner page, and in the window that Mission Control opens) and the sign-up window open your own mail app; they send nothing from here.
